Wildlife

Bear country

This is black-bear habitat. Bears are after food, not you — keep food secured and everyone stays happy.

  • Keep all food, coolers, trash and scented items inside and put away — never in cars or on the deck.
  • Bear boxes / storage: add where they are and what to use them for
  • Keep doors and low windows closed when you're out or asleep. Don't leave food out overnight.
  • If you see a bear: give it space, don't run, make yourself big and loud from a distance, and never get between a bear and its cubs or its food. Report any problem bear to us and, if there's an immediate safety threat, call 911.
