6. Acknowledgement of Risks

The Lodge is off-grid and in a remote mountain, bear-country setting, with a rough-road / over-snow approach and, seasonally, avalanche and wildfire risk. Guest and Guest’s entire party accept these risks.

The undersigned User hereby acknowledges and agrees that use of the Lodge has inherent risks and hazards. These risks and hazards include, but are not limited to, driveways, stairs, doorways, fences, trails, rough and unpaved or over-snow roads, avalanches, snowbanks, gates, parking areas, lightning, wind, rain, snow, mixed precipitation, wildfire and smoke, insects, wild animals (including bears), extreme temperatures, rocks, trees, fire and wood-burning appliances, or rivers and swift-moving water. User is assuming such risks and hazards based on User’s desire to use the facilities and amenities to engage in activities at the Lodge. User acknowledges that User may be subject to injury from such activities and use of the facilities and amenities, and that no form of preplanning can remove all of the danger to which User may be exposed in doing so. User further represents and warrants to Indemnitee that User has full knowledge of the nature and extent of all risks associated with engaging in activities at the Lodge and the use of equipment, including but not limited to: skiing, snowboarding, snowshoeing, camping, cooking, cross-country skiing, snowmobiling, dogsledding, hiking, fishing, backpacking, running, mountain biking, road cycling, rock climbing, rappelling, hunting, rafting, packrafting, kayaking, canoeing, swimming, horseback riding, use of a wood-fired sauna, as well as any other activity that User may engage in while visiting the Lodge.
