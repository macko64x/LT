The ski-in arrival

Cold Stream Road, over snow
Part of what makes Lost Trail, Lost Trail

Once the snow comes, the last stretch of canyon road isn’t plowed. You park at the winter lot and travel the final leg over snow — ski, sled or snowshoe, with your bags shuttled. It’s a genuine backcountry arrival, and it’s why the canyon stays as quiet as it does.

Provisions & fuel

Full grocery runs at Save Mart and Safeway; New Moon Natural Foods for organic and specialty. Fuel up in Truckee before you head up — there’s no gas past town.

Gear & rentals

Tahoe Mountain Sports, Truckee Sports Exchange and Paco’s Truckee Bike & Ski for rentals, tuning, backcountry gear and local trail beta. Tell us ahead and we’ll set the group up.

Coffee & breakfast

Coffeebar for the morning cortado; Squeeze In and Wild Cherries for a proper breakfast; Truckee Bagel Company to fuel a trail day.

Lunch & casual

Full Belly Deli for sandwiches, Burger Me for the group, and Best Pies when everyone’s hungry and undecided.

Dinner out

Moody’s Bistro in the historic Truckee Hotel, Pianeta for Italian, Cottonwood above town for the view — and Trokay when it’s a special night. Reservations help on weekends.

FiftyFifty Brewing and Alibi Ale Works for local pints; Bar of America on Commercial Row for a cocktail and live music.

Before you comeGood to know

Elevation & weather

You’re around 6,000 feet — drink extra water the first day and expect big day-to-night swings, even in summer. Pack layers and a rain shell any month.

Getting in

The canyon road is unpaved: AWD or 4×4 recommended in summer, and required plus over-snow travel in winter. Fuel and provision in Truckee.

Connectivity & power

Cell service is spotty in the canyon, but the lodge has Starlink wifi and off-grid solar with generator backup. Download maps offline before you leave town.

Bears & leave-no-trace

This is black-bear country: keep food inside and secured, and pack out what you pack in. Leave the canyon better than you found it — it’s why it’s still this good.

In an emergency

Call 911. The nearest hospital is Tahoe Forest Hospital in Truckee. Your booking packet has the exact address, access details and an on-site emergency contact.

Conditions

Sierra Avalanche Center for winter snow; Caltrans QuickMap or 511 for roads. Check both the night before and the morning of.
